Parachute System for Directional Sonobouy 
 
The Sonobuoy system broadly consists of a parachute, floating bag with surface 
electronic unit, sea water activated battery, cable reel and underwater sensor housing unit. 
The Sonobuoys are expendable unit and are launched from aircraft at designated area.  
For air launch of Sonobuoy, a parachute system is to be integrated to slow down the 
impact speed of Sonobuoy for safe entry into sea . The details of parachute system are as 
follows: 
 A single stage parachute. 
 Packed volume of the parachute: Cylindrical dia. 111 mm and length 30 mm 
 Two-point attachment of parachute lines. 
 Type: Square Canopy 
 Nominal Dia = 0.6 m 
 Shelf life of parachute system 10 years.
