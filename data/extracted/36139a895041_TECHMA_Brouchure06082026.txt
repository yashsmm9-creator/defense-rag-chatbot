 Introduction  The rapid emergence of autonomous technologies, artificial intelligence, advanced manufacturing, cyber capabilities, and space-based systems are fundamentally reshaping the character of warfare and national security. Nations across the world are witnessing paradigm shift from platform-centric defence systems to interconnected, intelligent, and autonomous ecosystems capable of faster decision-making, precision operations, and enhanced resilience.   The conference, on “Technology Management in the Era of Defence Autonomous Ecosystems” seeks to provide an interdisciplinary platform for researchers, policymakers, defence professionals, industry leaders, academia, and innovators to deliberate on the opportunities and challenges associated with managing emerging defence technologies. As nations strive towards self-reliance and technological sovereignty, the conference will also explore policy frameworks, governance mechanisms, and strategies for building an Atmanirbhar and resilient defence ecosystem. It seeks to encourage collaborative research, promote Industry - academia – government partnerships, and identify pathways for accelerating innovation and capability development in defence and strategic sector. The deliberations and recommendations emerging from this forum are expected to support informed decision-making and strengthen the nations’ preparedness for the rapidly evolving technological and geopolitical landscape of the twenty-first century.   This conference will provide a common platform to the professionals/ scientists from Ministry of Defence (Dept. of Defence R&D, Tri-Services, DPSUs), professionals from other research organisations (DAE, ISRO, CSIR etc), Industry and Academia for assimilation and exchange of ideas. It would facilitate research professionals to gain knowledge from experienced Scientists/ Engineers/ Academicians who are working extensively in various technological projects in R&D/ manufacturing in India and also present technical papers pertaining to the theme.

 About ITM   Institute of Technology Management (ITM) is located in the picturesque surroundings of Himalayan Ranges in Mussoorie (Uttarakhand). The core mandate of ITM is to impart state-of-the-art Technology Management training to the Scientists & Officers of DRDO and related training to Officers from the Indian Armed Forces. ITM has also been entrusted with the responsibility three-stage techno- managerial training for DRDS Officers. In addition, ITM conducts management courses and special customised courses for Technical, Admin and Stores cadres of DRDO. Participants for ITM courses are also drawn from the Defence PSUs. ITM plans and conducts approximately 35 structured courses & workshops and customised training in each Training Calendar Year for about 1500 professionals. The training is aimed to enhance the techno-managerial skills of the participants with a view to improve their competence and effectiveness in the execution of system development projects. This is achieved through conducting courses in the areas of Technology Management, Project Management, R&D Management, Quality Management, Product Development and Organisational Behaviour. This Institute, for more than 60 years, has made its mark by significantly contributing towards the effective deliverance of the core mandate enmeshed in the DRDO Vision for Excellence. Today ITM is geared up for providing customised solutions in Technology Management, Project Management and Material Management etc to DRDO technical clusters as well as to our armed forces.
