INDIGENOUS SONAR TECH DEPLOYED ON LATEST 
COMMISSIONED SHIPS
Hon’ble Prime Minister 
Narendra Modi, on 21 June 2026, 
commissioned two indigenously 
built Indian Navy ships in 
Kolkata: the stealth frigate INS 
Dunagiri and the anti-submarine 
warfare craft INS Agray. These 
naval warships have been 
designed by the Warship Design 
Bureau (WDB) and Defence Ship 
Production (DSP) and built by 
Garden Reach Shipbuilders & 
Engineers. The vessels feature 
over 75% indigenous content, 
including the sonars, which are 
developed by the Naval Physical 
& Oceanographic Laboratory 
(NPOL), Kochi.
NPOL is the backbone of 
India's underwater surveillance 
and anti-submarine warfare 
technologies, which plays the 
defining	 role	 in	 providing	 these	
newly commissioned warships 
with their core underwater 
perception capabilities. The two 
newly commissioned vessels—INS 
Dunagiri and INS Agray—feature 
state-of-the-art indigenous sonar 
suites designed and developed by 
NPOL.   
These systems are the "eyes 
and ears" of the naval vessels 
and designed to detect, classify, 
and track hostile submarines and 
underwater threats in the highly 
complex acoustic environment of 
the Indian Ocean region.
NPOL's	 specific	 technological 	
contributions across these ships 
include the following Sonars: 
INS Dunagiri (Stealth 
Guided-Missile Frigate)
As a frontline combatant, INS 
Dunagiri requires long-range 
target acquisition to maximize its 
weaponry. NPOL equipped it with 
HUMSA 2020.
HUMSA-2020—HUMSA-NG 
Mk II 
HUMSA-2020—HUMSA-
NG for P17A ships (HUMSA-NG 
MK II): The product support 
project during 2019-2022 was 
taken by DRDO for an already 
completed MM Project—HUMSA-
NG to design, develop and prove 
the system with new electronic 
architecture with enhanced 
features. The system was designed 
and developed by NPOL and 
manufactured by BEL, Bangalore. 
There are 16 orders for this system: 
P17A (7), Talwar (3), Talwar 
Additions (4), INS Rana (1), and 
INS Brahmaputra (1). The system 
has completed lab evaluation, 
FATS, pre-SATS and SATS and is 
already inducted and operational 
on board ships. 
INS Agray—Anti-Submarine 
Shallow Water Craft 
Operating in shallow littoral 
waters is acoustically complex 
due	 to	 wave	 reflections,	 reefs,	
and	coastal	traffic.	For	INS	Agray,	
NPOL developed customised, 
lightweight sonar variants. 
Shallow Water Sonar Suites—
Abhay PG 
Shallow Water Sonar Suites 
(SWCS)—Abhay PG is a compact 
hull-mounted sonar designed and 
developed	 by	 NPOL	 for	 fitment	
onboard 16 shallow water crafts by 
GRSE and CSL.

TEST & TRIALS
DRDO CONDUCTS SUCCESSFUL FLIGHT -TEST OF 
PINAKA LONG RANGE GUIDED ROCKET
The Defence Research & 
Development Organisation 
(DRDO) conducted a successful 
flight	 test	 of	 the	 Pinaka	 Long	
Range Guided Rocket (LRGR) 
at the Integrated Test Range 
(ITR), Chandipur, on 8 July 
2026. The rocket was tested for 
a	 user-defined	 minimum	 range	
of 60 km. Demonstrating all in-
flight	 manoeuvres	 as	 planned,	
the LRGR impacted on the target 
with textbook precision exactly 
following the predicted trajectory. 
All the deployed range instruments 
tracked	 the	 flight	 throughout	 its	
trajectory.
The Pinaka LRGR rocket is 
designed by Armament Research 
and Development Establishment 
(ARDE), Pune, in association with 
High Energy Materials Research 
Laboratory (HEMRL), Pune, with 
support from Defence Research 
and Development Laboratory 
(DRDL), Hyderabad, and Research 
Centre Imarat (RCI), Hyderabad. 
The	 flight	 trial	 was	 coordinated	
by ITR and Proof & Experimental 
Establishment (PXE). The rocket 
was launched from the in-service 
Pinaka launcher, demonstrating 
its versatility and providing launch 
capability of Pinaka variants of 
different	 ranges	 from	 the	 same	
launcher.
Hon’ble Raksha Mantri Shri 
Rajnath Singh congratulated 
DRDO, the Indian Army and the 
industry	 on	 the	 successful	 flight	
test. He described it as a major 
milestone in indigenous design 
and development capability for 
long-range guided rockets.
Defence Secretary and 
Secretary, Department of 
Defence R&D and Chairman, 
DRDO Shri Rajesh Kumar Singh 
closely monitored the trials and 
complimented all the teams 
associated with the successful test.
