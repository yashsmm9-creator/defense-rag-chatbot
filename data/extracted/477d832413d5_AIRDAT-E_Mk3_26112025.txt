AIRDATS-E  Mk3
The AIRDATS-E Mk3 Test system is used for the Hardware Software  
Integration testing of four numbers of Auxiliary Computer (AC) of LCA Air  
Force Mk2 and Verification and Validation of IFCS with ACs. Four Auxiliary  
computers shall collectively receive air data from all smart probes and process 
the raw signals (i.e., Static pressure, Total pressure, Temperature, AOA and  
AOSS).
AIRDATS-E MK3 is used for Hardware Software Integration (HSI) of Auxiliary  
computer with following functional requirements.
a. Capabilities to execute test scripts for testing Auxiliary Computer with  
strict adherence to timing, I/O mapping and functionalities.
b. Capabilities to introduce failures in simulations of the interfaces.
c.  Hard  real  time  capability  to  test  On-board  Flight  Program  (OFP)  
software of Auxiliary Computer.
Scope  of  ToT :   AIRDATS-E  Mk3  is  a  test  system  intended  for  Hardware-
Software Integration (HSI) of Auxiliary Flight computer of LCA-Mk2 and for  
progressive integration at ADA & Iron Bird, HAL. ADE has designed one unit of  
the  test  system  which  is  certified  by  CEMILAC  as  type1.  All  future  
procurements  of  AIRDATS-E  Mk3  test  systems  will  be  done  by  the  ToT  
partners. ToT partners should Build, Install, Operate, Maintain and provide  
operational support of AIRDATS-E Mk3 test systems including Comprehensive  
AMC.  The scope does not include ToT of the s/w residing in the test system.  
However, ADE will provide binaries to be installed in the system along with  
installation procedure.
The system has simulated sensors, actuators and other FCS Interfaces and  
will have provision to connect to real LRUs, fault injection features. It also has  
provision  to  interface  with  Flight  Dynamics  Simulator  (FDS)  in  Iron  Bird  
configuration.
