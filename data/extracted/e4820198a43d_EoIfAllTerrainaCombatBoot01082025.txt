All Terrain and Combat Boots 
All Terrain and Combat Boot developed by Defence Institute of Physiology and Allied Sciences (DIPAS) 
is a Light Weight boot, constructed on ergonomic principles with improved functional features for different 
terrain and shock absorption ability. It improves balance and stability during walking uphill and in real 
terrain conditions either with or without load. It reduces transfer of impact force to the lower extremities 
and minimizes risk of falls and overuse injuries. Reduction in energy expenditure delays onset of fatigue 
and enhances walking efficiency and mobility.
