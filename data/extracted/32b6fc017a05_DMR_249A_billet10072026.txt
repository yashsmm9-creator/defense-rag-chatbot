DMR-249A Grade Steel Billets for Naval Applications 
Billets are rolled product having rectangular cross section manufactured from cast ingots 
or continuous cast slabs. Billets are used as feedstock to manufacture bulb bars by rolling. 
The rectangular cross section of billets i.e. (width x thickness) ranges from m inimum-(80 x 
100) mm to maximum-(250 x 100) mm with requisite saw cut length to suit as feedstock for 
lowest section (P6) and highest section (P24) of bulb bars respectively. DMRL has 
optimized steel making parameters and finalized QAP to ensure  billets with improved 
metallurgical quality. The ToT seeking industry  has to develop the roll pass design on his 
own for converting the ingots to the billets of specific cross section by rolling to meet 
dimensional requirements. Billets have been accepted as feedstock for manufacture of 
bulb bars by the competent authority of Indian Navy and a Naval Construction Document: 
NCD 0249 Part -III Issue 1, April 2011 has been issued by Directorate of Naval 
Architecture (DNA), Integrated Headquarters (IHQ), MOD (Navy). DMR 249A Billets are 
currently in production at approved manufacturers for use as feedstock to manufacture 
bulb bars. 
 
Salient feature: 
The billets find use as feedstock to manufacture the finished product, namely, Bulb Bars 
which are used as hull stiffeners in naval construction by Indian navy/ Shipyards.

Min facility/Capability required at the time of submission of EoI: 
Steel making and casting facilities consisting of: 
• Electric Arc Furnace (EAF)/ Basic Oxygen Furnace (BOF), 
• Ladle Refining furnace (LRF)/Ladle Furnace (LF), 
• Vacuum Arc Degassing (VAD/VD) along with Ca-Si treatment and 
• Ingot/Continuous casting to make as cast ingots/slabs 
 
Billet/Blooming Mill consisting of: 
• Reheating furnace with high temperature capacity (1250°C) 
• Billet/ bloom making rolling mill 
• Cutting facility 
• Hoods/ furnace for slow cooling of rolled billets 
 
Final Testing/ Inspection/ Acceptance facilities consisting of: 
• Ultrasonic inspection 
• Surface grinding 
• Chemical analysis, gas content, macro-etching, hardness tester 
 
Requirement to issue Technology Absorption Certificate: 
The billets shall be subjected to the following acceptance tests and satisfactory 
compliance of same is essential requirement for issuance of the certificate. 
 
1. Chemical composition & Gas content 
2. Macro examination 
3. Inclusion Rating 
4. Ultrasonic examination 
5. Dimensional inspection & surface quality 
6. Surface hardness (for information only)
