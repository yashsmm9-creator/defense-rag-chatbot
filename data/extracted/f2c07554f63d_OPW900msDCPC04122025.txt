900ms Delay cum Pyro Cartridges (DCPC) 
HEMRL has developed several pyro cartridges to meet the requirement of missile programme. 
900ms DCPC is one of the delay pyro cartridges developed for carrying out ignition of auxiliary 
motors. The development is complete in all the aspects and comprehensive technology is ready 
for transfer for production. 
The schematic of 900ms DCPC delay pyro cartridge is as follows. 
 
 
            900 ms DCPC 
900ms DCPC delay pyro cartridge has electrical initiator, delay compos ition and pyrotechnic 
compositions. On provision of electrical impulse, 900ms DCPC provides suitable energy in 
quick time essentially required for ignition of henceforth elements. 900ms DCPC has been 
designed considering the requirement of redundancy as we ll as high reliability. The required 
raw materials for hardware fabrication as well as chemicals for preparation of pyrotechnic 
compositions are indigenized and available for taking up production by Govt./Pvt. agencies. 
Thus, the technology for manufacturi ng of 900ms DCPC delay  pyro cartridge is matured and 
ready for transferring as per the Govt. norms to suitable Govt./Pvt. agencies exhibiting 
explosive handling license.
